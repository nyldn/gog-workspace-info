NYLDN Fleet Workspace

Privacy notice

Updated 12 October 2026.

NYLDN Fleet Workspace is private automation operated by Chris Stephens for tasks he requests on accounts he is authorized to access. It is not a public service or an advertising product.

Google account access

Google OAuth permissions allow the gog command-line tool to access the Workspace services selected during consent. Depending on the task, this can include email and attachments, calendars, contacts, Drive files, documents, spreadsheets and other authorized Workspace records. Changes and email sends require a task that authorizes them.

Processing and storage

OAuth credentials are kept in protected credential storage on the computer performing the task. Requested content and task results can appear in local files, task histories, operational memory and configured backups.

Relevant content can be passed to the configured AI service to perform an authorized task. Processing and retention by that service depend on its terms and the operator's account settings. The workflow must not use Google data to train generalized AI models, for advertising, or for sale.

This informational website does not receive Google account credentials or Workspace content. Its hosting provider may process ordinary website request logs.

Control and removal

The operator controls stored task records, backup retention and connected accounts. Google access can be revoked in Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future authorized API access; it does not automatically delete files, task records or existing backups.

Contact chris@nyldn.com about access, retention or removal.

Google API use is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
