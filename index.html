NYLDN Fleet Workspace

Private Google Workspace automation operated by Chris Stephens. It uses the gog command-line tool to carry out requested tasks on Google accounts the owner is authorized to use.

Tasks can include finding email, preparing replies, managing calendars, and working with Drive files, documents and spreadsheets. Google account consent happens on Google's own pages.

This website provides application information. It has no sign-in form and does not collect Google credentials or Workspace content.
